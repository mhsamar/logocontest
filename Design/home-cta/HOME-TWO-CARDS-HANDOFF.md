# logocontest.bd home page: "Run a logo contest / Join as a designer" section

`home-two-cards.html` is the exact design of this section (HTML + CSS). Open it in a browser and hover the cards.

## What changes
- The two big white cards become two shorter (about 200px) coloured cards side by side.
- Left, **Run a logo contest**: maroon card, label "For business owners", title, one line, white button "Start a contest" (same link as today). On the right, four coloured letter tiles float and fan out on hover, with a gold "Ideas coming in" pill.
- Right, **Join as a designer**: cream-gold card, label "For designers", maroon title, one line, maroon button "Sign up as a designer" (same link as today). On the right, a small profile card with a QR code and a bouncing gold trophy; on hover the profile card straightens and grows.
- Both cards: the whole card is the link; on hover the card lifts, a light shine passes over it and the button arrow turns.
- Under 720px: the pictures are hidden; label, title, text and button stay.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no floating or bouncing.
- The letter tiles are decoration only (not real logos). Do not add numbers like "+24 ideas".
